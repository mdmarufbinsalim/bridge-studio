Your earbuds. Your Linux desktop. One network away.

BridgeAudio turns your phone into a wireless bridge: desktop audio flows out to your Bluetooth earbuds, and your earbuds' mic flows back in as a Linux microphone. No cables, no cloud, no account.

How it works

01

Install on your PC

One command sets up PipeWire routing and a bridgeaudio CLI. No manual audio configuration.

02

Run it

bridgeaudio starts listening on your local network and prints a QR code.

03

Scan it from your phone

Open the app, scan the code, and your desktop audio starts playing through whatever your phone is connected to.

Built for how it's actually used

Real audio device, not a hack

Creates an actual PipeWire virtual sink your desktop plays to — works with any app, not just ones BridgeAudio knows about.

Built for jittery Wi-Fi

Audio travels over UDP with a jitter buffer and loss concealment, so one dropped packet is a fraction of a frame, not a stutter.

Stays local

Desktop and phone talk directly over your LAN. No account, no cloud relay, no data leaving your network.

Stop it without hunting for the process

bridgeaudio stop finds and cleanly shuts down a running instance from any terminal.
